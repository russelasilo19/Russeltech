The Story

How I Got Here

Little did I know that something was awakening in me.

For a long time, I followed the script: get the grades, secure the license, find the safe job. I did the work, earned my Electronics Engineering degree, and became a Licensed Electronics Technician. But while the system provided the technical knowledge, it failed to provide the roadmap for financial independence or true contribution.

I realized that relying on a "safe job" was a gamble on someone else's terms. I had a burning desire to build a life of my own — to be financially independent, to make a meaningful impact, and to honor the people who gave me a second chance.

The problem? I had no capital and no mentors. I had to get resourceful. I stopped asking for permission and started investing in my own capability.

I took my engineering discipline — my attention to detail, my obsession with diagnostics, and my technical rigor — and applied it to the world of business strategy. I moved from the classroom to the boardroom. Apex Systems & Scale Strategy is the result of that resourcefulness. I don’t just build circuits anymore; I build the operational systems that allow leaders to scale their impact and secure their financial freedom.
